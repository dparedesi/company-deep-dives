The designation is the most legible fact in Palantir's 2026 record, because both sides of the same war behaved as if it were true. The war opened on February 28 with strikes that killed Iran's Supreme Leader, and it was still active at Day 231 in October. Across those months the Pentagon pushed Palantir's Maven Smart System from roughly 50,000 defense users to more than 100,000, formalized it as a program of record, and directed more sole-source purchases toward the company. The founding question of 2003 was whether the software that caught payment fraud could catch terrorists. By 2026 the company built on that question had reached a stranger status: war infrastructure, with its offices and data centers pinned on an adversary's target list.

**The central thesis**

Palantir sells Operational Sovereignty: control over the software layer through which an organization, and in the limit a state, senses, decides, and acts (developed fully in Chapter 9). The capability rests on three mechanisms. First, Apollo's environment-agnostic software management, which leaves the client, not the vendor, in control of where the software runs. Second, ontology-grounded platforms, Foundry, Gotham, and AIP, which give the enterprise one semantic model of its own operations that humans and agents act against. Third, a delivery model that embeds engineers with operators, keeping humans on the loop while automation compounds.

In 2026 the thesis stopped being an argument. A 100,000-user Maven runs at NATO's highest accreditation. The Army consolidated dozens of contracts into one ten-year platform agreement, and England's health service runs its national data platform on the stack. The sovereign AI line sells the same idea to governments as models, hardware, and control bought together. The standing question is who is granted such sovereignty, and under which legal and democratic constraints. That question left the seminar this year: two parliamentary select committees, a health-service break clause, and a foreign military's target list all took positions on it.
