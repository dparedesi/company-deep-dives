# Cover design prompt: The Memory Business

Use this prompt to generate a professional book cover for "The Memory Business: SanDisk's Full Cycle" by Daniel Paredes.

---

## Prompt for image generation agent

Create a professional non-fiction book cover for a deep analytical book about SanDisk Corporation, the flash memory company. The book covers SanDisk's complete corporate lifecycle: founding in 1988, IPO in 1995, acquisition by Western Digital in 2016, and re-emergence as an independent company in 2025.

### Visual concept

The cover should evoke the themes of memory, cycles, and technology. The central visual metaphor is a circular or cyclical motif representing SanDisk's "full circle" journey from startup to acquisition and back to independence. This cycle imagery should be integrated with subtle references to NAND flash memory: circuit patterns, silicon wafer textures, or abstracted memory chip geometries.

### Color palette

- **Primary:** Deep navy blue (#1a237e). Use this as the dominant background or base color. It should feel authoritative and corporate, like a serious financial or technology analysis.
- **Accent:** Vivid orange (#ff6f00). Use sparingly as a highlight color for key visual elements or the cycle motif. This represents the flash of electricity and SanDisk's brand energy.
- **Supporting:** Darker navy or near-black for depth contrast. White or light gray for text and fine details.

### Composition

- **Upper portion:** The title "The Memory Business" in large, clean serif typography (think Linux Libertine or similar). White or very light text against the deep blue background.
- **Center:** The cyclical visual element. This could be an abstract ring, a circuit-patterned circle, or an orbital motif suggesting return and renewal. The orange accent should highlight part of this element, perhaps a single arc or node breaking from the circle to suggest independence.
- **Lower portion:** The subtitle "SanDisk's Full Cycle" and author name "Daniel Paredes" in smaller, clean sans-serif type.

### Typography

- Title: Serif, elegant but modern. Not decorative or ornate.
- Subtitle: Sans-serif, lighter weight, clearly subordinate to the title.
- Author name: Sans-serif, same weight as subtitle or slightly bolder.
- No italics on the title. No quotation marks around the title.

### Mood

- Authoritative and analytical. This is a serious deep-dive analysis, not a lightweight business book.
- Technologically grounded. The reader should immediately sense this is about semiconductors and hardware, not software or internet services.
- Clean and minimal. Avoid clutter. The cover should read well as a thumbnail on Leanpub and Amazon.

### What to avoid

- Do not use the SanDisk logo or any trademarked company branding.
- Do not use photographs of products or people.
- Do not use generic stock imagery (handshakes, globes, ascending arrows).
- Do not make it look like a textbook or academic paper.
- Do not use more than three colors (deep blue, orange, white/light gray).
- Do not add gradients or gloss effects. Keep it flat and modern.

### Reference style

Think of the visual style of Princeton University Press or MIT Press economics/technology books: clean, geometric, confident, restrained. The cover should feel like it belongs in a serious library, not on a airport bookstore impulse-buy rack.

### Technical specs

- Aspect ratio: roughly 6 x 9 inches (standard US trade paperback)
- Resolution: at least 300 DPI for print
- Format: JPG or PNG
- The cover will be rendered in PDF and displayed on screen, so it needs to work at both full size and thumbnail size.

### Summary in one sentence

A deep navy blue book cover with an abstract cyclical motif in orange accent, clean serif title typography, and a restrained technology aesthetic that communicates serious analytical depth about flash memory and corporate strategy.
