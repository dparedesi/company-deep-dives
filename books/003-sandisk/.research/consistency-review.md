## Issues Requiring User Attention

### 1. Terminology Drift: Joint Venture Naming
**Severity:** Low
**Description:** The joint venture is referred to by multiple names across chapters:
- "Flash Forward" (Chapter 3, initial introduction)
- "Toshiba joint venture" (Chapters 4, 5, 6, 7, 8)
- "Western Digital-Kioxia joint ventures" (Chapters 8, 9, 10)
- "Flash Forward venture" (Chapter 4)

**Recommendation:** After introducing "Flash Forward" as the original name in Chapter 3, consistently use "Flash Forward" or "Flash Forward joint venture" throughout. This provides both historical accuracy and consistency.

---

### 2. Kioxia Ownership Description Inconsistency
**Severity:** Low
**Description:** The ownership structure is described slightly differently across chapters:
- Chapter 8: "Bain Capital: 51.11%, Toshiba: 30.5%, Hoya Corporation: 3%, Remainder: Other investors"
- Chapter 9: "Bain Capital owns 51.11% of Kioxia, giving the private equity firm controlling interest. Toshiba Corporation retains 30.5% ownership."
- Chapter 10: Mentions "3 percent owned by Hoya Corporation"

**Recommendation:** Standardize to use "approximately 3%" or "~3%" in Chapter 10 to match the precision of other chapters, or use exact "3%" in all chapters for consistency.

## Manufacturing Paradox Consistency

The "manufacturing paradox" concept is consistently described across chapters 8, 9, and 10:
- Western Digital owns 50% of NAND manufacturing through Kioxia joint ventures
- WD no longer sells NAND flash products
