## Overview

The most significant development in SanDisk's post-acquisition history occurred on **February 24, 2025**, when Western Digital completed the spin-off of its Flash business as an independent, publicly-traded company called **Sandisk Corporation** (note the lowercase "d"). This event fundamentally reshapes the "post-acquisition legacy" narrative — the 2016 acquisition was effectively undone nearly nine years later, with SanDisk returning to independence.

The spin-off creates two focused entities: Western Digital (HDDs only) and Sandisk Corporation (NAND flash only). However, Western Digital retains its flash joint ventures with Kioxia, creating an ongoing strategic connection to the NAND business.

## Detailed Findings

### 1. SanDisk Spin-Off from Western Digital (Completed February 24, 2025)

**Status:** COMPLETED

- **Completion Date:** February 24, 2025
- **New Legal Name:** Sandisk Corporation (lowercase "d")
- **Stock Ticker:** SNDK (relisted on NASDAQ)
- **CEO:** David Goeckeler (formerly CEO of Western Digital, moved to lead the spin-off)
- **Headquarters:** Milpitas, California (original SanDisk HQ)

**Financials (2025, pre-spin-off pro forma):**
- Revenue: $7.355 billion
- Net Income: -$1.64 billion (loss)
- Employees: 11,000

**Rationale for Spin-Off:**
Western Digital stated the separation would allow each company to pursue focused strategies:
- Sandisk Corporation: Pure-play NAND flash,专注于闪存存储解决方案
- Western Digital: Pure-play hard disk drives (HDDs)

### 2. Western Digital Post-Spin-Off (2025)

**Status:** NEWLY FOCUSED ENTITY

- **New CEO:** Irving Tan (succeeded David Goeckeler, who moved to lead Sandisk)
- **Revenue:** $9.52 billion (2025)
- **Focus:** HDDs exclusively
- **Continued Flash Exposure:** Western Digital retains its 50% stake in the Western Digital-Kioxia joint ventures for NAND flash manufacturing

This creates an unusual structure where Western Digital is an "HDD-only" company but still owns half of major NAND manufacturing capacity through its Kioxia JV.

### 3. Kioxia Ownership Structure (Relevant to SanDisk's Legacy)

**Status:** INDEPENDENT (but strategically connected)

Kioxia (formerly Toshiba Memory) remains a key piece of the flash memory ecosystem that SanDisk helped build:

- **Ownership Structure:**
  - Bain Capital: 51.11%
  - Toshiba: 30.5%
  - Hoya Corporation: 3%
  - Remainder: Other investors

- **Leadership:** Nobuo Hayasaka, President and CEO
- **Spin-Off Date:** June 2018 (from Toshiba)
- **IPO Status:** Filed for IPO in 2023 but has not yet listed (as of April 2026)

**Connection to SanDisk Legacy:**
Kioxia owns what was once SanDisk's joint venture manufacturing capacity in Yokkaichi, Japan. Western Digital (and now indirectly Sandisk Corporation) maintains these manufacturing partnerships.
