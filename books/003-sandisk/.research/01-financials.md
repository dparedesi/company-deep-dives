# SanDisk Financial History - Research Findings

**Research Date:** April 11, 2026
**Domain:** Chapter 2, 5, 6, 7 - Financial History covering 1988 founding through 2016 WD acquisition

---

## Overview

SanDisk's financial history spans 28 years from founding in 1988 through acquisition by Western Digital in May 2016. The company went public on November 8, 1995, and operated as an independent public company for 20.5 years before being acquired. This research covers SanDisk's complete financial lifecycle: IPO and early public years, revenue trajectory, profitability patterns, the Western Digital acquisition, stock performance, valuation history, cash flow and balance sheet evolution, capital allocation (dividends and buybacks), earnings surprises, and analyst coverage.

**Critical Data Limitation:** SanDisk was acquired by Western Digital in May 2016, and the SanDisk brand was spun off as a new independent entity in February 2025 under the same SNDK ticker. Most financial APIs and data sources return data for the post-2025 "Sandisk Corporation" rather than historical 1995-2016 SanDisk data. This creates significant gaps in retrievable historical financial information.

### 3. Profitability Margins

**Confirmed Data Points (January 2016):**
- **Gross Profit:** $2,368.3 million
- **Gross Margin:** 42.6% ($2,368.3 / $5,564.9)
- **Operating Income:** $758.9 million
- **Operating Margin:** 13.6% ($758.9 / $5,564.9)
- **Net Income:** $388.5 million
- **Net Margin:** 7.0% ($388.5 / $5,564.9)

**Context:** These January 2016 margins represent a period of NAND industry stability before the 2016-2017 downturn. The 42.6% gross margin indicates reasonable pricing power in the flash market, while 13.6% operating margin shows the heavy R&D and depreciation burden typical of NAND manufacturers.

### 4. Western Digital Acquisition Details

**Acquisition Announcement:**
- **Date:** October 2015
- **Initial Offer Value:** $19 billion (combination of cash and stock)

**Acquisition Completion:**
- **Date:** May 12, 2016
- **Final Transaction Value:** $16 billion
- **Price per Share:** $85.50 (85.50 in cash and stock)
- **Premium:** Approximately 30% over pre-announcement trading price

**Deal Structure:**
- Western Digital acquired all outstanding shares of SanDisk
- Combination of cash and WD stock
- Created combined storage company with both HDD and NAND flash capabilities

**Rationale from WD:**
- Diversify beyond declining HDD market into growing SSD/flash storage
- Gain SanDisk's NAND flash manufacturing capability through Toshiba JV
- Achieve R&D and manufacturing synergies
