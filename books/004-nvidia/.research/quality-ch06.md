## Specific Missed Facts (THIN sections)

### 1. Open-Source CUDA Alternatives
**Location:** CUDA software moat section
**Missed:**
- Unified Acceleration (UXL) Consortium: Intel, Google, ARM, Qualcomm, others formed to create open-source CUDA alternative
- Triton language: OpenAI-developed Python-based GPU programming language gaining research adoption
- Both represent emerging structural threats to CUDA's long-term dominance

### 2. Startup Ecosystem Detail
**Location:** Barriers to entry section
**Missed:**
