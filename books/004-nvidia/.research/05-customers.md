# NVIDIA Customers and Use Cases

Research Period: Through February 2026 (Q4 FY2026)

---

## Executive Summary

NVIDIA's customer base has transformed dramatically from a gaming-focused GPU supplier to the foundational infrastructure provider for the AI industrial revolution. The company's Data Center revenue reached $193.7 billion in FY2026 (up 68% year-over-year), driven by hyperscaler demand, enterprise AI adoption, and sovereign AI initiatives. NVIDIA's customer concentration is heavily weighted toward large cloud providers and technology companies, with the company reporting that a significant portion of revenue comes from a small number of hyperscalers.

**Key Customer Segments:**
- Hyperscalers (cloud providers): Primary revenue driver
- Enterprise AI: Rapidly growing adoption across Fortune 500
- Automotive: Strategic partnerships with major OEMs
- Healthcare & Life Sciences: Specialized AI deployments
- Government & Sovereign AI: National AI infrastructure projects
- OEM/Server Partners: Dell, HPE, Lenovo, Supermicro

**Risks:**
- Customer concentration creates dependency risk
- Hyperscaler vertical integration efforts (custom accelerators)

## 11. Customer Retention and Revenue Dynamics

### 11.1 Net Revenue Retention

NVIDIA benefits from strong revenue retention due to:

- Multi-generational partnerships (e.g., Meta, hyperscalers)
- Increasing AI compute demands as models grow
- Platform stickiness (CUDA ecosystem)
- Software subscriptions (NVIDIA AI Enterprise)

### 11.2 Churn Risk

**Potential Churn Drivers:**
- Hyperscaler vertical integration (developing custom accelerators like AWS Trainium, Google TPU)
- Competitive pressure from AMD (MI series) and Intel (Gaudi)
- Regulatory actions that could impact partnerships
- Customer-specific capex timing shifts

**Mitigating Factors:**
- Performance leadership (Blackwell, Rubin roadmaps)
- CUDA ecosystem moat
- Full-stack solution (hardware + networking + software)
- Rapid innovation pace

## 18. Conclusion and Outlook

NVIDIA's customer base has evolved from a gaming GPU supplier to the foundational infrastructure provider for the AI industrial revolution. The company's relationships with hyperscalers are both its greatest strength (revenue scale, long-term partnerships) and greatest risk (customer concentration, vertical integration threats).

**Key Trends:**
1. Enterprise AI adoption is accelerating beyond hyperscalers
2. Sovereign AI projects create new national-scale customers
3. Automotive and healthcare verticals are scaling
4. Agentic AI and physical AI represent emerging use cases
5. Customer concentration risk remains elevated but is gradually diversifying

**Strategic Position:**
NVIDIA's full-stack approach (hardware + software + networking) and CUDA ecosystem create significant switching costs for customers, supporting strong retention and pricing power. The company's ability to maintain performance leadership through Blackwell, Rubin, and future architectures will be critical to customer retention.
