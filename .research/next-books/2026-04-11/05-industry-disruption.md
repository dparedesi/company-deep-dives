Research date: 2026-04-11

## Methodology

This report identifies companies driving or benefiting from major industry disruptions across eight thematic categories. Research draws from:

1. **Existing research synthesis** - Cross-referencing findings from search trends (01), recent IPOs (04), and book performance/adjacent opportunities (07)
2. **Industry media monitoring** - TechCrunch AI/defense/space coverage, Defense News, SpaceNews, Bloomberg Technology
3. **Demand signal triangulation** - Combining market cap, trading volume, media coverage, and retail investor interest
4. **Thematic cluster analysis** - Identifying companies that stand out within disruption narratives

**Excluded:** Palantir (PLTR), Rocket Lab (RKLB), SanDisk (SNDK) - already published.

**Ranking criteria:** Demand signal (high/med/low), "why now" catalyst strength, evidence quality, reader profile clarity, unique angle potential.
