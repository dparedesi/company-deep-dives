## Methodology

This report consolidates findings from 8 parallel research angles:

1. **Search trends** (01) — Yahoo Finance most-active tickers, trending stocks, search growth
2. **News media** (02) — Coverage frequency across Reuters, TechCrunch, CNBC, The Verge, Bloomberg
3. **Investor interest** (03) — Trading volume, retail enthusiasm, Seeking Alpha engagement
4. **Recent IPOs** (04) — Companies that went public 2024-2026 with information asymmetry
5. **Industry disruption** (05) — Companies driving thematic shifts in AI, defense, space, energy
6. **Content gaps** (06) — Analysis of existing book coverage and missing treatments
7. **Book performance** (07) — Adjacent opportunities to existing portfolio
8. **Wildcard** (08) — Contrarian, hidden champion, and non-obvious picks

**Scoring framework (1-5 scale):**
- **Search demand** (25% weight) — Trading volume, search trends, retail investor attention
- **News momentum** (20% weight) — Media coverage frequency and intensity
- **Investor interest** (20% weight) — Retail + institutional enthusiasm, trading patterns
- **Content gap** (20% weight) — Absence of quality book-length analysis
- **Narrative potential** (15% weight) — Compelling story angle, founder narrative, contrarian hook

**Signal bonus:** +0.5 for companies appearing in 4+ research angles (indicates cross-theme validation)

**Composite score** = weighted sum + signal bonus (max 5.5)
