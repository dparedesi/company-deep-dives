# Book performance analysis and adjacent opportunities

## Methodology

This analysis examines the existing portfolio of three published deep-dive books (Palantir, Rocket Lab, SanDisk) to identify patterns in subject selection, map adjacent companies benefiting from the same macro tailwinds, and rank candidates for future books based on reader demand signals.

**Data sources:**
- Yahoo Finance (real-time quotes, statistics, performance, comparable companies, news)
- Seeking Alpha (analyst coverage, article volume, ratings, sentiment)
- Google Finance (supplementary data for recently IPO'd companies)

**Analysis framework:**
- Why Palantir is trending (catalyst decomposition)
- Cross-book pattern recognition (what the three books share)
- Defense tech ecosystem mapping (Palantir peers, partners, competitors)
- Space ecosystem mapping (beyond Rocket Lab)
- Semiconductor/hardware ecosystem mapping (beyond SanDisk)
- Demand signal scoring (analyst coverage, retail interest, news volume, price momentum)

Research date: April 11, 2026.

## Why Palantir is trending

Palantir (PLTR) is the strongest demand signal in the current portfolio. The book "The Palantir Enigma" benefits from multiple concurrent catalysts that have driven the stock from ~$85 (late 2025) to ~$128 (April 2026), a 1-year return of +73%, 3-year return of +1,631%, and 5-year return of +528%.

### Key catalysts

1. **Maven AI designated a Pentagon "program of record."** This is the single most important catalyst. Palantir's Maven Smart System, originally built for Project Maven (AI-powered battlefield intelligence), was formally designated as a program of record by the U.S. Department of Defense. This means it transitions from pilot/contract status to a permanent, budgeted line item with sustained multi-year funding. It triggered a +6.94% single-day surge. This is structural, not cyclical.

2. **Golden Dome missile defense shield.** Palantir and Anduril are collaborating on the software layer for the proposed "Golden Dome" national missile defense system. This is a potential multi-billion-dollar program that would cement Palantir as the operating system of U.S. defense infrastructure.

3. **AIP transition to production.** Palantir's Artificial Intelligence Platform (AIP) has moved from pilot projects (bootcamp phase) to production agentic workflows. Enterprise customers are now deploying autonomous AI agents built on Palantir's ontology layer, which UBS described as a "moat." This is the commercial (non-defense) growth driver.

4. **S&P 500 inclusion and Nasdaq transfer.** Palantir joined the S&P 500 and transferred its listing to Nasdaq, forcing passive index funds to buy the stock and increasing institutional visibility.

5. **Dedicated Palantir ETFs.** Four dedicated ETFs exist (PLTE, PLTW, PLTU, YPLT). This is extremely unusual for a single company and signals extraordinary retail investor demand. No other company in our research set has this level of retail product development.

6. **Analyst sentiment: overwhelmingly bullish.** On Seeking Alpha: 24 earnings revisions up, 0 down. Growth grade A, Profitability A+. Valuation grade D- (expensive, but that does not stop demand). Headlines include "Palantir: The World Changed When Silicon Valley Met Defense" and "Palantir: Winner of the AI War."

7. **Partnership expansion.** Five-year extension with Stellantis, expanded consulting pact with Bain & Company, and ongoing expansion of government contracts.

### Financial profile (as of April 2026)
- Market cap: ~$312-350B
- Revenue (TTM): $4.48B, YoY growth +56.18%
- Profit margin: 36.31%
- Forward P/E: 98.76
- Seeking Alpha followers: 442,000+

## Cross-book pattern analysis

The three published books share structural characteristics that define the "deep-dive" formula:

| Attribute | Palantir | Rocket Lab | SanDisk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sector | Defense tech / AI | Space launch / services | Semiconductors / storage |
| Stage | High-growth, profitable | High-growth, pre-profit | Turnaround / spinoff |
| Market cap (at research) | ~$300B+ | ~$38B | ~$20B (est.) |
| Retail investor interest | Extreme (442K SA followers) | Very high | Moderate |
| Narrative hook | "Secretive company goes mainstream" | "New Zealand to orbit" | "Resurrection of a dead brand" |
| Macro tailwind | AI + defense modernization | Space economy expansion | Data storage demand |

**Shared patterns:**
- All three are technology companies with a compelling origin story
- All have strong founder/CEO narratives (Karp, Beck, Harari/Eliyahu)
- All sit at the intersection of hardware and software
- All have a "contrarian" or "underdog" element that makes for interesting narrative
- All have enough public financial data for deep analysis but are under-analyzed in book form
- All benefit from macro tailwinds that are still early-inning (AI, space, data growth)

**What makes a strong deep-dive candidate:**
1. A compelling founder or corporate narrative (not a boring conglomerate)
2. Massive retail investor interest (high Seeking Alpha/Yahoo Finance follower count)
3. A clear macro tailwind that readers want to understand
4. Sufficient complexity to justify a book (not a one-story company)
5. Adjacent to existing books (cross-sell to the same reader base)

## Defense tech ecosystem mapping

Palantir operates in a rapidly expanding defense technology ecosystem. The U.S. defense budget is shifting from legacy contractors (Lockheed, Northrop, RTX) toward "Silicon Valley meets Pentagon" companies. Key players:

**Tier 1 (direct competitors/partners to Palantir):**
- **Anduril** (private) - Autonomous defense systems, Lattice OS. Palantir's partner on Golden Dome. The most closely associated company in the public mind.
- **C3.ai (AI)** - Enterprise AI platform, direct competitor in government AI contracts. Smaller, earlier-stage, controversial short-target.
- **CrowdStrike (CRWD)** - Cybersecurity with growing government footprint. Adjacent rather than direct competitor.

**Tier 2 (defense tech beneficiaries):**
- **Kratos Defense (KTOS)** - Unmanned drone systems, hypersonic vehicles. 1-year return +201%. Market cap ~$17B. Revenue $1.35B (TTM). Profitable but thin margins (1.63%). Direct Palantir ecosystem peer.
- **AeroVironment (AVAV)** - Tactical unmanned aircraft systems, loitering munitions. Market cap ~$10.5B. Major defense tech pure-play.
- **Northrop Grumman (NOC)** - Legacy prime, but leading in hypersonics and B-21 Raider. Market cap ~$105B.

**Tier 3 (platform/enabling companies):**
- **Palantir's ontology layer** connects to virtually every defense contractor. Any company building defense software or autonomous systems is either a customer or partner.

## Space ecosystem mapping

Rocket Lab operates in the "New Space" economy. Beyond launch, the ecosystem includes satellite communications, lunar infrastructure, space data, and orbital services.

**Tier 1 (Rocket Lab direct peers):**
- **Firefly Aerospace (FLY)** - Recently IPO'd. Launch vehicle (Alpha), lunar lander (Blue Ghost). Market cap ~$3-5.7B. Revenue ~$30.8M (Q3 2025), still deeply unprofitable. Direct competitor to Rocket Lab's launch and space systems business.
- **Intuitive Machines (LUNR)** - Lunar lander company. First private company to land on the Moon. Market cap ~$3.6B. Revenue $210M (TTM). $180.4M NASA contract for IM-5 mission. 1-year return +219%. Very strong narrative.
- **AST SpaceMobile (ASTS)** - Space-based cellular broadband network (direct-to-smartphone). Market cap ~$35B. Revenue only $70.9M (TTM), deeply unprofitable (-$342M net income). But 1-year return +313%, 3-year +1,723%. BlueBird satellite constellation launching. Massive insider ownership (27.7%). Projected earnings growth 109.4% annually.

**Tier 2 (space services/infrastructure):**
- **Redwire (RDW)** - Space infrastructure, in-space manufacturing. Market cap ~$1.9B.
- **Planet Labs (PL)** - Earth observation data. Market cap ~$8.6-12B.
- **York Space Systems (YSS)** - Small satellite manufacturing. Recently public.

**Tier 3 (nuclear/space power):**
- **Oklo (OKLO)** - Advanced fission reactors for data centers and space applications. Market cap ~$10B. Sam Altman-backed. Zero revenue. US DOE award for nuclear fuel recycling. 1-year return +32%, but highly volatile (52-week range $17-$194). Adjacent to both space and AI themes.

## Top 5 candidates for next deep-dive books

### 1. Anduril Industries (private, but IPO expected)

- **Company:** Anduril Industries
- **Ticker:** Private (IPO anticipated 2026-2027)
- **Demand signal:** Maximum. Anduril is the most talked-about defense tech company after Palantir. Co-founded by Palmer Luckey (Oculus VR founder). Valued at $28B+ in private markets. Direct Palantir partner on Golden Dome. If/when it IPOs, it will be the defense tech event of the decade.
- **Why now:** Golden Dome partnership with Palantir. Lattice OS becoming the standard for autonomous defense. Ukraine/Russia conflict proving the value of autonomous systems. IPO likely within 12-18 months. Every Palantir book reader would want this.
- **Evidence:** Anduril is mentioned in virtually every Seeking Alpha article about Palantir. The "Palantir-Anduril axis" is the dominant narrative in defense tech. Luckey's personal story (fired from Facebook, built Oculus, sold to Meta for $2B, then founded Anduril) is a bestseller-ready narrative.
- **Reader profile:** Palantir book readers, defense tech enthusiasts, AI/autonomy investors, tech biography fans.
- **Suggested angle:** "The Oculus Founder's Second Act: How Palmer Luckey Is Building the Military's Operating System." Focus on the Luckey narrative, the Silicon Valley-goes-to-Pentagon theme, and the Lattice OS platform.

**Risk:** Company is still private. Book would need to be timed around IPO. Could instead do a "defense tech landscape" book covering Anduril + Kratos + others.

### 2. AST SpaceMobile (ASTS)

- **Company:** AST SpaceMobile, Inc.
- **Ticker:** ASTS (NasdaqGS)
- **Demand signal:** Very high. 1-year return +313%. Market cap $35B. 1,126 employees. Building the first space-based cellular broadband network. BlueBird satellite constellation actively launching.
- **Why now:** SpaceX IPO news is driving massive interest in the space sector (ASTS surged 10.3% on SpaceX news alone). BlueBird 7 launch imminent. Amazon rumored to be considering a takeover of ASTS's partner. Apple investors face risk from satellite partnerships. The "smartphone-to-satellite" narrative is the hottest topic in telecommunications.
- **Evidence:** Yahoo Finance headline: "AST SpaceMobile Flies 10.3% Ahead of BlueBird 7 Launch." Barron's: "Iridium, Viasat Stocks Surge. Why a Bid for a Satellite Rival Is Good News." Multiple "stocks that could 10x" articles feature ASTS. Insider ownership 27.7% (very high alignment). Projected earnings growth 109.4% annually.
- **Reader profile:** Rocket Lab book readers, telecommunications investors, space economy enthusiasts, growth stock investors seeking the "next SpaceX."
- **Suggested angle:** "The Satellite in Your Pocket: How AST SpaceMobile Is Building the First Space-Based Cellular Network." Focus on the Abel Avellan founder story, the BlueBird constellation, the AT&T/Verizon partnerships, and the "direct-to-smartphone" revolution.

### 3. Kratos Defense and Security Solutions (KTOS)

- **Company:** Kratos Defense & Security Solutions, Inc.
- **Ticker:** KTOS (NasdaqGS)
- **Demand signal:** High. 1-year return +201%. 3-year return +605%. Market cap ~$17B. Revenue $1.35B (TTM). 4,300 employees.
- **Why now:** Unmanned drone systems and hypersonic vehicles are the future of warfare, proven in Ukraine. Kratos builds jet-powered tactical drones (Valkyrie) and propulsion systems for missiles and hypersonics. Defense budget is shifting from manned to unmanned systems. Direct Palantir ecosystem peer (both serve DoD with software-defined capabilities).
- **Evidence:** Canaccord Genuity maintains Buy rating with $125 price target. Kratos is consistently listed among defense tech peers alongside Palantir, AeroVironment, and Anduril. Revenue growing, approaching profitability. The "loyal wingman" drone concept is becoming a program of record.
- **Reader profile:** Palantir book readers, defense tech investors, military technology enthusiasts.
- **Suggested angle:** "The Robot Air Force: How Kratos Is Building the Unmanned Future of Aerial Warfare." Focus on the Valkyrie loyal wingman program, the shift from manned to unmanned combat, and the hypersonics opportunity.

### 4. Intuitive Machines (LUNR)

- **Company:** Intuitive Machines, Inc.
- **Ticker:** LUNR (NasdaqGM)
- **Demand signal:** High. 1-year return +219%. YTD +40%. Market cap ~$3.6B. Revenue $210M (TTM). 525 employees.
- **Why now:** First private company to land on the Moon (IM-1, February 2024). $180.4M NASA contract for IM-5 mission just announced. NASA's Artemis program is accelerating lunar exploration. Intuitive Machines is the only private company with proven lunar landing capability. The "lunar economy" narrative is just beginning.
- **Evidence:** Stifel raised price target to $22. Yahoo Finance: "Intuitive Machines (LUNR) has seen a surge following a significant $180.4 million NASA contract for its IM-5 lunar mission." Company provides delivery services, data transmission, and infrastructure-as-a-service for the Moon. Directly adjacent to Rocket Lab's space thesis.
- **Reader profile:** Rocket Lab book readers, space exploration enthusiasts, NASA/Artemis followers, investors in the "lunar economy."
- **Suggested angle:** "The First Company on the Moon: How Intuitive Machines Is Building the Lunar Economy." Focus on the IM-1 landing story, the Nova-C lander, the NASA CLPS program, and the vision for lunar infrastructure.

### 5. Oklo (OKLO)

- **Company:** Oklo Inc.
- **Ticker:** OKLO (NYSE)
- **Demand signal:** Moderate-high. Sam Altman-backed. Market cap ~$10B. 120 employees. Zero revenue but massive narrative power.
- **Why now:** AI data centers need enormous amounts of electricity. Nuclear is the only zero-carbon baseload option that can scale. Oklo is developing small modular reactors (SMRs) specifically for data center power. US DOE award for nuclear fuel recycling. Joint agreement with newcleo. Sam Altman connection gives it crossover appeal (AI + energy).
- **Evidence:** Texas Capital Securities initiated coverage with Buy and $138 price target (stock at $65.56, implying 110% upside). Analyst target range $14 to $175 (extremely wide, showing high uncertainty but also high potential). People who watch OKLO also watch CRWV (CoreWeave) and NBIS (Nebius) - all AI infrastructure plays.
- **Reader profile:** AI infrastructure investors, energy transition enthusiasts, nuclear power advocates, crossover between Palantir (AI) and Rocket Lab (advanced technology) reader bases.
- **Suggested angle:** "The Nuclear Renaissance: How Oklo and Sam Altman Plan to Power the AI Revolution." Focus on the SMR technology, the data center energy crisis, and the Sam Altman visionary narrative.

## Summary of demand signals by ecosystem

**Defense tech (Palantir-adjacent):** Anduril (#1, if IPO), KTOS (#3), AVAV (#6), RCAT (#12), NOC (#19), LMT (#22). This is the strongest ecosystem for reader cross-sell from the Palantir book.

**Space (Rocket Lab-adjacent):** ASTS (#2), LUNR (#4), FLY (#7), PL (#9), RDW (#11), YSS (#24), VOYG (#25). The space economy narrative is the second strongest ecosystem, benefiting from SpaceX IPO excitement and Artemis program milestones.

**AI infrastructure (crossover):** Oklo (#5), CRWV (#10), CEG (#15), VST (#16), IONQ (#17), NBIS (#21), RGTI (#23). This is the broadest ecosystem, touching energy, compute, and quantum. The "AI needs power and compute" narrative is the dominant macro theme of 2025-2026.

**Semiconductors (SanDisk-adjacent):** This ecosystem is more mature and well-covered. A SanDisk-adjacent book would need a strong narrative hook (e.g., a specific company's turnaround or a niche semiconductor subsector).
