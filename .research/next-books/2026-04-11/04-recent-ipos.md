# Next books research: recent IPOs and newly public companies

Research date: 2026-04-11
Agent: recent IPOs

---

## Methodology

This report focuses on companies that have recently gone public (IPO'd in last 12-18 months) or are upcoming IPO candidates with high demand for deep-dive analysis.

**Signals combined to rank candidates:**

1. **IPO recency**: Companies that listed between late 2024 and early 2026 benefit from "new to public markets" information asymmetry
2. **Price action post-IPO**: Dramatic gains or losses create investor demand for explanatory analysis
3. **Lockup expiration catalysts**: 6-month post-IPO lockup expirations create secondary selling pressure and narrative opportunities
4. **Pre-IPO buzz**: Companies generating significant media coverage before listing
5. **SPAC-to-business quality**: Companies that went public via SPAC but have delivered real businesses

**Excluded**: Palantir (PLTR), Rocket Lab (RKLB), SanDisk (SNDK) — already published.

## Top candidates

### 1. Firefly Aerospace (FLY)

**Demand signal:** High

**Why now:** Firefly IPO'd in early 2026 and is the newest publicly-trued space launch company. Direct competitor to Rocket Lab with both launch vehicles (Alpha) and lunar landers (Blue Ghost). The "New Space duopoly" narrative (Rocket Lab vs. Firefly) creates natural cross-sell from the existing Rocket Lab book.

**Evidence:**
- Market cap: ~$3-5.7B (per existing research)
- Revenue: ~$30.8M (Q3 2025), deeply unprofitable but early stage
- Blue Ghost lunar lander selected for NASA CLPS missions
- Alpha launch vehicle reaching commercial operations
- Direct Rocket Lab competitor in both launch and space systems

**Reader profile:** Rocket Lab book readers looking for the competitive analysis, space industry professionals, investors seeking "the next Rocket Lab"

**Suggested angle:** "The New Space Race: Firefly vs. Rocket Lab and the Battle for Low-Earth Orbit"

### 2. Astera Labs (ALAB)

**Demand signal:** Very high

**Why now:** Astera Labs IPO'd in early 2025 and became the "picks and shovels" play for AI data centers. The company makes connectivity solutions (PCIe switches, retimers) that are essential for GPU clusters. Stock surged post-IPO as investors realized every NVIDIA GPU needs Astera's chips to communicate. The company is the semiconductor infrastructure play that most retail investors didn't know existed.

**Evidence:**
- IPO pricing: March 2025 at strong premium
- Positioning: Essential connectivity for AI/GPU clusters
- Customer base: NVIDIA, AMD, major cloud providers
- Post-IPO momentum: AI infrastructure theme drove strong early performance

**Reader profile:** Investors who understand NVIDIA but want the "next layer down" in the stack, semiconductor industry professionals

**Suggested angle:** "The Glue Holding AI Together: How Astera Labs Became the Hidden Backbone of the Data Center"

### 3. Rubrik (RBRK)

**Demand signal:** Medium-high

**Why now:** Rubrik IPO'd in April 2024 as a cloud data security and zero-trust data management platform. Founded by Bipul Sinha (former Lightspeed partner), the company combines cyber security with data management. Adjacent to Palantir's enterprise data thesis — Rubrik secures what Palantir analyzes. The company is profitable and growing, making it a "safer" tech IPO in a volatile market.

**Evidence:**
- IPO date: April 2024
- Sector: Cloud data security, zero-trust data management
- Founder narrative: Bipul Sinha, VC-turned-founder
- Profitability: Rare among recent tech IPOs
- Adjacent to Palantir's enterprise data platform thesis

**Reader profile:** Palantir book readers (enterprise data thesis crossover), cybersecurity investors, CIOs evaluating data platforms

**Suggested angle:** "Zero Trust, Zero Compromise: How Rubrik Is Reinventing Data Security for the AI Era"

### 4. Ibotta (IBTA)

**Demand signal:** Medium

**Why now:** Ibotta IPO'd in early 2025 as a retail rewards and cashback platform. The company represents the "retail media" wave — brands paying to reach consumers at the point of purchase. Stock has been volatile post-IPO as investors debate whether it's a tech company or a promotions play. The information asymmetry around the business model creates demand for explanatory analysis.

**Evidence:**
- IPO timing: Early 2025
- Business model: Cashback rewards + retail media network
- Volatility: Post-IPO price swings reflect uncertainty about classification
- Retail media tailwind: Brands shifting spend from traditional advertising to point-of-purchase

**Reader profile:** Retail investors curious about consumer tech, marketing professionals, investors in the "retail media" theme

**Suggested angle:** "The Tech Company That Pays You: Inside Ibotta's Retail Media Revolution"

### 5. Reddit (RDDT)

**Demand signal:** Very high

**Why now:** Reddit IPO'd in March 2024 at $34/share and became one of the most-watched newly public stocks. The platform sits at the center of retail investor culture (memestocks, crypto discussions) and is now monetizing through data licensing (the "Google deal"). The stock has been volatile with dramatic swings based on user growth and monetization narratives. The irony — the platform that moves stocks is now a stock itself — creates endless media coverage.

**Evidence:**
- IPO date: March 2024 at $34/share
- Price action: Highly volatile post-IPO with dramatic swings
- Data licensing: $60M/year Google deal for AI training data
- Cultural position: Ground zero for retail investor sentiment
- User base: 73M daily active users, 267M weekly

**Reader profile:** Retail investors who use Reddit, social media analysts, investors in the "social platform data monetization" thesis

**Suggested angle:** "The Meme Stock Goes Public: Inside Reddit's High-Stakes Bet on Monetizing Culture"

**Reader profile:** Robotics industry professionals, AI infrastructure investors, manufacturing leaders

### 7. ServiceTitan (private, IPO filed 2025)

**Demand signal:** Very high

**Why now:** ServiceTitan filed for IPO in 2025 and is the largest pending offering in the software-as-a-service space. The company provides management software for trades businesses (plumbing, HVAC, electrical). It's a massive under-digitized market ($500B+ annual spend) and ServiceTitan is the clear leader. The "blue collar software" narrative is compelling and undercovered in tech media.

**Evidence:**
- IPO filing: 2025
- Market: Trades software, $500B+ addressable market
- Position: Clear category leader with first-time founder story
- Undercovered: "Blue collar software" gets less media attention than consumer tech
- Founders: Ara Mahdessian and Vahe Kuzoyan ( compelling immigrant founder narrative)

**Reader profile:** SaaS investors, trades business owners, software industry analysts

**Suggested angle:** "Software for the Trades: How ServiceTitan Is Digitizing the Last $500B Industry"

### 8. Plaid (IPO expected 2026)

**Demand signal:** Very high

**Why now:** Plaid is the fintech infrastructure company that powers apps like Venmo, Robinhood, and Cash App. It attempted to sell to Visa for $5.4B in 2021 (blocked by regulators), and is now planning an IPO. The company sits at the center of the "banking-as-a-service" and "open banking" movements. Every fintech app uses Plaid to connect to bank accounts.

**Evidence:**
- Blocked Visa acquisition: $5.4B deal (2021) created massive awareness
- Position: Infrastructure layer for fintech (connects apps to 12,000 financial institutions)
- IPO timeline: Expected 2026
- Network effects: More apps = more financial institutions = stronger moat
- Customer base: Robinhood, Venmo, Cash App, most major fintechs

**Reader profile:** Fintech investors, financial services professionals, API economy enthusiasts

**Suggested angle:** "The Pipes of Finance: How Plaid Became the Invisible Backbone of Fintech"

## Secondary offering catalysts (lockup expirations)

Companies that IPO'd 6+ months ago approaching/d past lockup expiration create selling pressure and information asymmetry:

- **Reddit (RDDT)**: Lockup expired September 2024, creating volatility
- **Astera Labs (ALAB)**: 6-month lockup early investors in September 2025
- **Ibotta (IBTA)**: Lockup expiration mid-2025

Lockup expirations are covered in financial media but rarely understood by retail investors, creating educational demand.

## SPACs that delivered

While SPAC boom (2020-2021) produced many failures, several companies have built real businesses:

- **AST SpaceMobile (ASTS)**: SPAC'd 2021, now $35B market cap. Space-based cellular network.
- **Rocket Lab (RKLB)**: SPAC'd 2021, successful launch and space systems business.
- **SoundHound AI (SOUN)**: SPAC'd 2022, voice AI platform. Surged in 2025-2026 on AI theme.
- **Berkshire Grey (BGRY)**: Robotics for warehouses, taken private by but illustrates SPAC-to-real-business path.

These are covered elsewhere in research but worth noting as "SPAC success stories" for the broader thesis.
